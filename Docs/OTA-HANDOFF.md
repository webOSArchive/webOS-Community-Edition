# OTA hand-off: CE 3.1.0 → 3.2.0 (Path A)

Written 2026-11-20 for the agent taking this over. State at commit `cbdb396` on `main`,
everything pushed. The plan and every finding are in [OTA-PLAN.md](OTA-PLAN.md): read §2.0
(the measurement), §2.0a (the first run on a device) and §2.2 (`ce-ota-finish`). This page
is where it stands and what I would do next.

## In one paragraph

An unsigned payload that turns a 3.1.0 rootfs (600070) into 3.2.0 is generated by a tool,
and the job that finishes an update on the first boot (`ce-ota-finish`) is baked into the
image from 600108. A 600070 → 600109 payload was applied **by hand to a running TouchPad**
and the device came up as 3.2.0. **The update ramdisk, `mmipkg`, `PmUpdater`, signing and
the OTA client have not been tried at all.** 600110 is the current image; its payload is
built and has not run on a device.

## The test device

## How the by-hand run goes

1. Pre-flight: `device-check.py` against the 600070 JAR. Expect no changed file but
   `/usr/lib/ipkg/status`, and md5 of `/usr/lib/ipkg/info/org.webosarchive.ce-files.md5sums`
   equal to `manifest-inputs.json`'s `preflight.md5` (`adb25d87…` for 600070).
2. `novacom put` the ipk to `/media/internal/ce-ota/`, check its md5.
3. On the device: unpack `control.tar.gz` to `/tmp`; `mount -o remount,rw /` **and
   `/boot`**; run `preinst`; `ar p … data.tar.gz | tar -C / -xvzf -`; run `postinst`;
   `sync`. `/` will not remount read-only (replaced binaries are still open): reboot.
4. Before the reboot, `device-check.py` against the new JAR and compare with the
   pre-flight listing: only the customization layer, `status` and directories no tar names
   may differ.
5. After the reboot read `/var/log/ce-ota-finish.log`,
   `/var/luna/preferences/ce-ota-finish*`, `/var/log/reports/librdx/`. The job takes about
   two minutes after boot.

novacom notes: pass scripts on stdin (`novacom run file://bin/sh <<EOF`), not as `sh -c`
arguments; the device has no `stat`, no `sha256sum`; `ipkg compare_versions` exits 1 for
true.

## Open, in the order I would take them

1. **Run 600110 end to end on a fresh 600070 device.** Nothing in 600110 has had a clean
   run: the installer switch, the sorted token check and the 4 KB-boundary logo search.
   Check after the first boot: the log says the logo was written *and verified*; all five
   apps "reinstalled and verified"; `org.webosarchive.photos.service` answers on the public
   bus (`luna-send -P -n 1 palm://org.webosarchive.photos.service/x '{}'` must say
   "Unknown method", not "Service does not exist"); no crash report.
2. **The LunaSysMgr crash.** On the updated device LunaSysMgr died in `fclose(NULL)` in
   `CardWindowManager::markFirstCardDone()` when the first card was minimized. The marker
   `/var/luna/preferences/used-first-card` was present and writable, yet LunaSysMgr neither
   saw it at start nor could open it. **Cause unknown.** On the 600110 run, minimize a card
   on the first boot and see whether it repeats. Whatever the cause, the missing check at
   `LunaCE/Src/lunaui/cards/CardWindowManager.cpp:1306` should be fixed (two lines; it means
   a LunaSysMgr rebuild and a bake). I held it back to see first whether the crash repeats.
3. **Does a flashed 3.2.0 device have Photos' service on the bus?** The stock preload pass
   is `app-install`, which runs plain `ipkg`, and on the updated device that left the
   service unregistered. If a flashed device has the same gap it is an image bug apart from
   the OTA (the photo editor cannot save; Import vCards drops contact photos). One
   `luna-send` on any flashed 3.2.0 TouchPad answers it.
4. **`/boot`.** It is its own partition, mounted read-only on a running device, and `tar`
   exits 2 on `/boot/boot-images.tar.gz`. Under `mmipkg` a failing data tar aborts the
   update. Read `ota.sh`, `pre-update` and `post-update` (in the rootfs under
   `/usr/share/ota-scripts/`) for what the ramdisk mounts, then confirm in A0. If `/boot` is
   not writable there: leave `/boot` out of the payload and have `ce-ota-finish` place the
   file (it already reads it for the logo).
5. **The two payload paths under `/var`** (`/var/imwrap.sh`,
   `/var/luna/preferences/used-first-card`). `/var` is its own volume. Whether the ramdisk
   mounts it under `/rootfs/var` is unknown; if not, the files land hidden on the root
   volume and `ce-ota-finish` must place them. Same reading and the same A0 answer as (4).
6. **A0, the bench rehearsal of the armed flash** (OTA-PLAN §2.3) with a trivial payload.
   This is the largest unknown left and nothing above replaces it. It needs the arm path
   in `../webos-update-exploration`, which is that repo's work.
7. **Comparing two device listings.** `device-check.py` compares a device with an image;
   the convergence test (§1.3) wants an updated device against a *flashed* one. Both
   listings are saved as `device.json`; the comparison is a few lines, not written.
8. **`/` was read-write for the whole first boot after the update**, read-only on the
   second. Not `ce-ota-finish`. Preware 1.9.22 installed in that boot and its scripts
   remount `/`: the suspect, not confirmed. Look at the mount state during the first boot
   of the 600110 run.
9. **`/` at 84% after the update.** Measure a flashed 600110 for comparison.
10. **AT&T.** No 3.2.0 AT&T image exists. Its payload needs one, diffed against 600071.
    The origin list for 600071 is already in the image.
11. **Never tested:** the feed-leftover step with a device that really installed the feed
    Videos, Calculator or Media TLS on 3.1.0; a device whose owner changed a seeded file;
    a low battery at the logo step; `/media/internal` in USB drive mode during the job.
12. **Not looked at on a screen:** launcher layout after an update (a device keeps its
    saved layout), the keyboard, Email, the boot logo itself. Ask the user; do not judge a
    UI from logs.

Not mine to decide, and still open in OTA-PLAN §1.4: signing, the manifest (tier field,
target binding), staging on `/media/internal`, the server gate. The payload's
`manifest-inputs.json` carries what the signer needs.

## Things that will bite

- **A bake needs the user's word.** Source changes "for the next bake" are fine; running
  `bake.py` is not, until asked. A bake must contain everything finished.
- **Never rebuild a JAR for a mark that was shared.** 600105 went to other developers.
  600108, 600109 and 600110 have not left this machine.
- **`ce-ota-finish` knows no version.** It compares the image it runs in with the origin
  lists. A new origin image means a new list (`make-payload.py finish-list`), nothing else.
- **A flashed device does nothing in that job**: no `ran-first-use` at boot means it writes
  both flags and exits. Keep that property.
- **A flashed device is not the rootfs tar.** The Doctor adds `hp.tar`'s four customization
  ipks. `hp.tar` is identical in 600070 and 600110; check that again for any new pair.
- **The NVRAM write is real.** `trenchcoat` rewrites the whole 4 MB partition, tokens
  included. The job saves it to `/var/ce-ota-nvram-backup.img` first and says in its log
  how to restore.
- **The OTA client, server and keys live in `../webos-update-exploration`.** Its
  `OTA-3.1.0.md` is the reference. `Docs/OTA-STRATEGY.md` here is stale.
- Mail is waiting on Microsoft's re-verification (about 2026-11-24): the client id in
  `com.palm.service.oauth/providers.json` may change, which means one more bake before a
  release payload.
